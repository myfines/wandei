# Moving-anchor Sturmian realizer scan — 2026-09-29

## Status

Exploratory computation and a proposed standalone target. This is **not** a proof of Collatz and the numerical scan is not a certified theorem.

The motivation comes from the final-dyadic-plateau lemma. A high final plateau has an accelerated valuation word with digits in `{1,2}` that is generated by a near-critical rotation. When the plateau is only `O(log H)` long, the accumulated Archimedean correction is exponentially small in the word length, so the carry word differs from a fixed-intercept mechanical word at at most very few positions (using any effective polynomial irrationality bound for `log_2 3`).

This reduces one important subproblem to the arithmetic size of the least positive integer realizing a finite critical mechanical/Sturmian valuation word when the intercept is allowed to move.

---

## 1. Critical slope

Let

\[
\omega=\log_2(3/2),\qquad \alpha=1+\omega=\log_2 3.
\]

For an intercept \(\rho\), define the binary mechanical word

\[
c_i(\rho)=\lfloor (i+1)\omega+\rho\rfloor-\lfloor i\omega+\rho\rfloor\in\{0,1\}.
\]

Turn it into an accelerated Collatz valuation word

\[
a_i=1+c_i\in\{1,2\}.
\]

For fixed length \(L\), varying \(\rho\) produces exactly \(L+1\) distinct Sturmian factors.

---

## 2. Exact least-realizer congruence used in the scan

For a finite valuation word \(a_0,\ldots,a_{L-1}\), let

\[
A=\sum_i a_i.
\]

Write the affine iterate as

\[
3^L x+C=2^A y,
\]

with

\[
C_0=0,\qquad C_{j+1}=3C_j+2^{A_j},\qquad A_j=\sum_{i<j}a_i.
\]

Requiring the terminal accelerated state \(y\) to be odd gives

\[
3^Lx+C\equiv2^A\pmod{2^{A+1}}.
\]

Therefore the starting odd integer is pinned to one residue class modulo

\[
2^{A+1},
\]

namely

\[
\boxed{x\equiv(2^A-C)3^{-L}\pmod{2^{A+1}}.}
\]

The scan takes the least positive representative of this class for every length-\(L\) Sturmian factor and then minimizes over the \(L+1\) factors.

The values fluctuate, as expected from continued-fraction structure, but they are consistent with growth close to the full modulus scale

\[
2^{\alpha L}.
\]

A random-residue heuristic over only \(L+1\) possible factors would suggest a smallest representative on the order of

\[
\frac{2^{\alpha L}}{L},
\]

which is broadly compatible with the observed trend. This heuristic is **not** a proof and should not be treated as one.

## 4. Proposed standalone target

A useful theorem would be a uniform moving-intercept lower bound of the form

\[
\boxed{
\min_{\rho}\operatorname{Realizer}_L(\rho)
\ge
\frac{2^{\alpha L}}{L^C}
}
\]

for some fixed constant \(C\), or even any lower bound

\[
\log_2 R_{\min}(L)\ge(\alpha-o(1))L.
\]

This is much more precise than saying vaguely that the critical mechanical word is not realized by one fixed positive integer. It asks for a quantitative anti-concentration result **uniform over all moving intercepts**.

For a plateau whose starting height is \(H\), such a theorem would imply

\[
L\le\frac{\log_2H}{\alpha}+O(\log\log H),
\]

up to the finite number of local carry defects caused by the small `+1` correction.

---

## 5. Why this is plausibly number-theoretic

The infinite fixed-intercept problem is adjacent to the known 2-adic conjugacy/Sturmian literature (Bernstein--Lagarias; López--Stoll) and to p-adic complexity/Subspace-Theorem phenomena. The hard point here is stronger and quantitative: the intercept is allowed to change with the dyadic layer, so qualitative irrationality of one fixed infinite Sturmian 2-adic number does not by itself give the required bound.

The finite realizer is an arithmetic placement problem inside a modulus of size approximately

\[
2^{\alpha L}=3^L.
\]

This is the same scale at which the current moving-anchor/EOC approaches report their central unresolved obstruction.

## 6. Short-plateau carry rigidity

If a plateau has length

\[
L\le C_0\log H,
\]

then

\[
E_L\le\frac{L}{3H\ln2}
\]

is exponentially small in \(L\).

Let

\[
D_j=\lfloor\theta_0+j\omega\rfloor,
\qquad
C_j=\lfloor\theta_0+j\omega+E_j\rfloor.
\]

The two can differ only when \(\{\theta_0+j\omega\}\) lies within \(E_L\) of an integer boundary. If two distinct indices \(j,k\le L\) were both sensitive, then

\[
\|(j-k)\omega\|<2E_L.
\]

Any effective linear-form-in-logarithms bound gives a polynomial lower bound

\[
\|q\omega\|\gg q^{-B}
\]

for some effective \(B\), while \(E_L\) is exponentially small in \(L\). Hence, for sufficiently high bands, there can be at most one sensitive cumulative-carry index.

At the digit level this means that the true carry word differs from a fixed-intercept mechanical word by at most one local adjacent transposition `01 -> 10` (or a terminal one-bit change). The final dyadic crossing contributes one additional forced defect (`carry=1`, actual extra valuation `b=0`).

Thus high short plateaus reduce to a **constant-defect moving Sturmian realizer problem**.

---

## 7. What remains open

The scan does not prove the proposed exponential lower bound. Existing qualitative bounded-drift and Sturmian results show that no single fixed positive seed can realize an infinite critical mechanical word, but that is weaker than a uniform quantitative lower bound over all finite intercept factors.

This moving-intercept anti-concentration statement is now a concrete target that can be attacked independently of the rest of the Collatz dynamics.
