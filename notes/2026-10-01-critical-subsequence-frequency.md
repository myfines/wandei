# Critical subsequential frequency transcendence in the bounded-run branch

Status: exact compactness/algebraic consequence of the six-lane sampled normal form and the critical sampled subsequence. Not a Collatz proof.

Assume a hypothetical sampled escape tail has eventually bounded odd-run counts

\[
q_j\le Q.
\]

Each sampled first-return block is then one of finitely many types

\[
r=(q,d),\qquad 0\le q\le Q,\quad d\in\{1,2,3,4,5,6\},
\]

with logarithmic principal increment

\[
\Delta_r=q\omega-d,
\qquad
\omega=\log_2(3/2).
\]

For a prefix of J sampled blocks, let

\[
f_r^{(J)}=\frac1J\#\{j<J:r_j=r\}
\]

be its empirical block-frequency vector. Then

\[
\frac{H_J}{J}
=
\sum_r f_r^{(J)}(q_r\omega-d_r).
\]

Earlier sampled criticality gives an infinite subsequence \(J_n\to\infty\) such that

\[
\frac{H_{J_n}}{J_n}\to0.
\]

Because the block alphabet is finite, the empirical frequency vectors lie in a compact simplex. Hence every sequence \(f^{(J_n)}\) has a convergent subsubsequence. Write its limit as \(f=(f_r)_r\). Passing to the limit gives

\[
\boxed{
\sum_r f_r(q_r\omega-d_r)=0.
}
\]

Define

\[
\bar q=\sum_r f_rq_r,
\qquad
\bar d=\sum_r f_rd_r.
\]

Since every \(d_r\ge1\), we have \(\bar d\ge1\), so the critical identity implies \(\bar q>0\) and

\[
\boxed{
\omega=\frac{\bar d}{\bar q}.
}
\]

Now \(\omega=\log_2(3/2)=\log_2 3-1\) is transcendental: if it were algebraic then \(\log_2 3=1+\omega\) would be algebraic; it is neither rational (unique factorization excludes \(2^a=3^b\)) nor algebraic irrational (Gelfond--Schneider applied to \(2^{\log_2 3}=3\)).

Therefore the coordinates of \(f\) cannot all be algebraic. Indeed, if every \(f_r\) were algebraic, then both \(\bar q\) and \(\bar d\) would be algebraic and \(\omega=\bar d/\bar q\) would be algebraic, contradiction.

Thus:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Every limiting empirical block-frequency vector obtained along a critical sampled subsequence has at least one transcendental coordinate.}
}
\]

## Why this strengthens the earlier frequency obstruction

The earlier algebraic-frequency obstruction assumed that global asymptotic block frequencies exist. The present statement removes that assumption in the bounded-run branch.

A hypothetical survivor may make its empirical frequencies oscillate forever, but compactness still produces limit points along the critical subsequence, and every such critical limit point must contain genuinely transcendental frequency data.

Hence eventual boundedness of \(q_j\) forces both:

1. **prime refresh:** for every fixed P, P-smooth cofactors have density zero, so large prime factors occur on a density-one set of sampled times;
2. **frequency transcendence:** every critical empirical-frequency limit point has at least one transcendental coordinate.

So the bounded-run survivor cannot be generated by any mechanism whose critical subsequential frequency limits are algebraic (in particular periodic, eventually periodic, rational-frequency automatic, or primitive-substitution regimes).

## Remaining gap

This still does not exclude an arbitrary Collatz orbit: deterministic integer dynamics can in principle generate transcendental limiting frequencies. The next bridge should use the arithmetic prime-refresh/order-clock constraints to restrict which critical frequency limit points are realizable, rather than relying on symbolic low complexity alone.
